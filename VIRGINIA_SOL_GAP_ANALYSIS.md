# Virginia 3rd Grade SOL Gap Analysis Framework

## Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive gap analysis between the **Virginia 3rd Grade Standards of Learning (SOL)** and the current **IEP Learning Application** content. The analysis covers Reading/English Language Arts (2024 SOL), Mathematics (2023 SOL), and Science (2018 SOL).

### Key Findings:
- **Reading/ELA**: Current coverage ~5-10% (CVC-level only, far below 3rd grade)
- **Mathematics**: Current coverage ~15-20% (Number sense 20-39, basic addition only)
- **Science**: Current coverage ~20-25% (Force/motion and simple machines only)

### Student Context:
- **Current Reading Level**: Kindergarten (CVC words)
- **Current Math Level**: Counting 20-39, single-digit addition
- **Grade Level**: 3rd Grade
- **Diagnosed Needs**: Autism, learning disabilities, IEP accommodations

#### ❌ **What We're Missing:**

### 3.FFR.3 Foundations for Reading (Phonics & Word Recognition)

#### **3.FFR.3.A - Vowel Teams and R-Controlled Vowels** ❌ NOT IMPLEMENTED
**SOL Requirement**: Decode and encode words with vowel teams (ai, ay, ea, ey, eigh) and r-controlled patterns (ar, er, ir, or, ur)

**Current Status**: ZERO coverage
- No vowel teams (rain, play, eat, they, sleigh)
- No r-controlled vowels (car, her, bird, corn, hurt)

**Gap Impact**: CRITICAL - These are essential 3rd grade phonics patterns

**Recommended Content to Add**:
1. Vowel team units:
   - Long A: ai (rain, train), ay (play, day)
   - Long E: ea (eat, read), ee (see, tree), ey (key, monkey)
   - Long I: ie (pie), igh (night, light)
   - Long O: oa (boat, goat), ow (snow, grow)
   - Diphthongs: oi (coin), oy (toy), ou (out), ow (cow)
2. R-controlled vowel units:
   - AR words (car, star, park)
   - ER words (her, fern, teacher)
   - IR words (bird, girl, first)
   - OR words (corn, horn, storm)
   - UR words (hurt, turn, nurse)

**Estimated Development**: 40-60 hours for full implementation
